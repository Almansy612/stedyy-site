Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Stedy, the weight-progression tracker for iPhone. Effective 25 November 2026.

In short

  • This page is for people in Washington State and Nevada, whose laws give extra protection to "consumer health data". It adds to our Privacy Policy.
  • Without an account, Stedy sends us no data about your body or training.
  • If you sign in, a backup that includes your workouts and body details is stored in your account, only to give it back to you.
  • We never sell this data, and never use it for advertising.

What we collect, and why

We collect consumer health data only if you sign in to an optional account. Stedy then stores a backup in your account that includes:

  • your training: workout dates, exercises, sets, weights and reps, how long a session took, how it felt, and whether you ticked creatine;
  • your body details, if you entered them: body weight and its history, height, sex, date of birth and build.

We use it for one purpose: to keep a backup you can restore on a new iPhone or after reinstalling the app. We don't use it for anything else.

Your photos, your Measurements and Posture readings and your Assistant conversations stay on your iPhone and are never collected. If you turn on Apple Health, Stedy writes your finished workouts to the Health app on your iPhone; it doesn't read your health data, and nothing from Health is sent to us.

Where it comes from

Only from you: what you log and enter in Stedy.

What we share, and with whom

We don't share consumer health data with third parties, and we don't sell it. One service provider, Supabase, stores the backup for us as a processor, under a data processing agreement, and may use it only to provide that storage. We have no affiliates.

Your rights

  • Know and access: you can ask whether we hold consumer health data about you and get a copy. Everything in the backup is also visible in the app itself.
  • Withdraw consent: sign out, or delete your account, at any time.
  • Delete: in Stedy, tap your photo at the top left of Home, open your profile, and under Account tap Delete account. This deletes your account and its backup from our provider.

To use any of these rights, or to appeal a decision we made about a request, email abdallah@stedyy.com. We'll reply within 45 days. If you're in Washington and unhappy with the result of an appeal, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General.
