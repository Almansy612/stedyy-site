The Assistant

The Assistant, part of Stedy Premium, answers questions about your training using Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence). Its answers are generated automatically and can be wrong, so check anything important against your own logs. They are general training guidance, not medical advice.

The Assistant is the only feature that needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence: iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 or later, running iOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on (see Apple's requirements). Everything else in Stedy, free and Premium, works on every iPhone the app supports.

Your data and backups

What you log is stored on your iPhone. If you sign in, Stedy also backs it up to your account after each workout and brings it back when you sign in on an iPhone; our Privacy Policy explains what the backup holds. Without an account, deleting the app deletes your data, and we can't recover it for you. Whether you sign in or not, you can use Export backup in Preferences to keep your own copy.
